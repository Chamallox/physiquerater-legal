Physique Rater

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy explains what Physique Rater collects, why, and the choices you have. The app has no account. Your photos, results and profile are kept on your iPhone.

1. Data the app collects

  • Photos you take or import, stored on your iPhone and sent for analysis only with your permission.
  • Profile details: birth year, gender, height, weight and goal. Your first name, if you give it, never leaves your iPhone.
  • Apple Health: height and weight, only if you allow it.
  • Purchases: which subscription is active, under a random ID.
  • Usage data: screens viewed, features used, errors, device model, iOS version and approximate location (country or city, from your IP address), under a random ID.
  • Install data: how you found the app, such as an ad, with technical data like IP address. The app does not use your advertising identifier.

2. How it is used

  • To estimate your body fat and muscle grades from your photos, and to draw one illustration of your goal.
  • To unlock what you paid for and give each iPhone one free scan.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.
  • To measure which ads lead to installs and purchases.

Your photos, body measurements, results and Health data are never used for advertising or analytics, and never sold.

3. Service providers

Physique Rater works with a small number of providers, each only for the job below:

  • AI provider: receives your photos and profile details for one analysis or one illustration. It does not use them to train its models, and may keep them for up to 30 days to prevent abuse before deleting them.
  • Server hosting: passes each request to the AI provider and keeps nothing.
  • Subscription management: records your subscription status. Payments are made through Apple.
  • Analytics: receives usage data.
  • Ad attribution: receives install and purchase events, and may share them with the ad network that showed an ad.
  • Apple: payments, Apple Health, and a device check that stores two yes or no flags per iPhone for the free scan.

4. Retention

Data on your iPhone stays until you delete it. The server stores no photos, measurements or results. Usage and install data are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above.

5. Your rights and choices

  • Withdraw permission to send photos in Settings, Privacy.
  • Delete everything with Settings, Delete All Data, or by deleting the app.
  • Change Apple Health access in the Health app.
  • Ask for a copy or deletion of usage and install data by email. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

6. Security

Requests are encrypted and signed, and the app can be locked with Face ID or your passcode. No system is perfectly secure, which is why your data stays on your iPhone wherever possible.

7. Children

Physique Rater is for adults 18 and over and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. International users

Providers may process data in the United States, the European Union or other countries, with safeguards required by law.

9. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
